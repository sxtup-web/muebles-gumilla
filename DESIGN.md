## Brand Identity
Warm, welcoming, artisanal, professional. References: Muuto, Article, HAY. Interaction and typography polish level: Apple and Stripe. AVOID generic e-commerce templates. Use warm neutrals (beige, cream, wood brown, off-white) with desaturated forest green or graphite accents. Avoid pure cold whites.

## Typography
- Headings: Lora (warm, crafted serif)
- Body: Inter (highly legible sans-serif)

## Layout
- Generous spacing, lots of whitespace.
- Photography is the protagonist.
- Soft micro-interactions on hover.
